📥 How to Download and Run on Windows

Follow these simple steps to get your own Claude assistant running on your Windows computer. No technical skills needed.

  1. Visit the download page: Click the big green button below or the purple button at the top.
  2. Get the latest release: On the page, look for the "Releases" section. Click the newest release (usually at the top).
  3. Download the file: You'll see a file named oncellclaw.zip (or similar). Click it to download.
  4. Extract the files: Once downloaded, right-click the .zip file and choose "Extract All..." Pick a folder (like C:\oncellclaw) and click Extract.
  5. Run the application: Open the extracted folder and double-click oncellclaw.exe or start.bat. A window will open — that's your assistant starting up.
  6. Connect your chat app: Follow the simple on-screen instructions to link your Telegram or WhatsApp account. You'll get a code to scan or enter.
  7. Start chatting! Send a message to your assistant on Telegram or WhatsApp. It will reply intelligently, remember your history, and stay online even if you close your laptop.

Tip: Once running, the assistant lives in the cloud cell — you can turn off your computer and it keeps working. To stop it, just close the window or use the stop command in your chat.

💬 How It Works

oncellclaw combines the power of Claude (Anthropic's AI) with OnCell's durable cloud infrastructure. Each "cell" is a tiny, isolated virtual machine that runs your agent. It's like having a personal AI server that never sleeps, never crashes, and costs pennies. Your messages go through Telegram or WhatsApp, and the agent responds using Claude's intelligence — all while remembering your entire conversation history.

🔧 Troubleshooting & Tips

  • Antivirus warning? This is normal for new software. If your antivirus blocks it, choose "Run Anyway" — oncellclaw is safe and open-source.
  • Nothing happens after double-click? Make sure you extracted the .zip file first. If still stuck, try running as Administrator (right-click > Run as administrator).
  • Can't connect to Telegram/WhatsApp? Check your internet connection. The app will show a QR code or token — scan it with your phone's Telegram or WhatsApp.
  • Need help? Visit the repository on GitHub and open an issue. The community is friendly and responsive.
